Who we are

Anno's One Fine Day is a Kenyan non-profit organisation using the power of creative arts and education to unlock potential, build confidence and create pathways to opportunity for children and young people from underserved communities in Kenya.

Based in Kibera, one of Nairobi's most vibrant and densely populated communities, our Anno’s One Fine Day Art Centre provides a safe, inclusive and inspiring space where young people can explore, dream, create, learn and imagine new possibilities for their lives.

The Anno’s One Fine Day Art Centre was completed and inaugurated in January 2024, in cooperation with the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ) and the German Development Cooperation (GIZ). Twenty staff and volunteers work regularly at the Centre.

“We believe that every child has a voice, and we create an enabling environment where they can express it in their own unique way. We ignite artistic dreams; and these dreams, our children's dreams, are our nation's future.”

Krysteen Savane, Founder and Director

What we teach

Our creative education programme engages more than 500 children and young people every week across eight artistic disciplines.

Visual ArtsAfrican & Contemporary DanceBalletDrama & Theatre FilmMusicAcrobaticsCreative Writing

Through these disciplines young people do more than learn artistic skills. They develop self-confidence, discipline, teamwork, communication, critical thinking, resilience and self-expression, skills that shape their education, relationships, livelihoods and futures.

Alongside the arts we run regular workshops on life skills, drugs, water and sanitation, health, hygiene, sexual and reproductive health, mental health and environmental awareness. These are subjects often overlooked but vital for young adults.
