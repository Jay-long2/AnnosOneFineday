Our story

We believe every child and young person deserves the chance to discover their talents and build self-confidence through creative expression.

Our programme lets children and adolescents explore their artistic abilities and use the arts to better understand themselves, their environment and the world they live in.

Our impact

Learning creative skills shapes a person's path in life. It builds the self-confidence to choose a career and plan a life on their own terms.

Many young people who joined our workshops call the experience a milestone in the decisions they made and the bolder paths they chose.
