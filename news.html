Krysteen Savane knighted with France's Order of Arts and Letters

Our founder and director was awarded the insignia of Chevalier de l'Ordre des Arts et des Lettres — Knight of the Order of Arts and Letters — one of France's highest cultural distinctions. The French Ambassador to Kenya, Arnaud Suquet, presented the award at a ceremony held at the Arts Centre in Kibera on 9 February 2026, attended by diplomats, artists and friends of the Centre.

The honour recognises a career spanning more than twenty years in Kenya's creative industry, and the Centre's role in building cultural ties between Kenya and France — including the French Embassy's support for our ballet studio.
